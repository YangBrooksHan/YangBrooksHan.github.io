I'm Yang. I train foundation models that learn to see, imagine, and act — today humanoid foundation models at Delta Intelligence, and before that video generation foundation models for Vidu at ShengShu.

My roots are in computational mathematics and AI × Science at Peking University, then years as a senior engineer on national-scale communication networks. Both taught me to see intelligence — natural or artificial — as a dynamical system shaped by how information flows.

One question runs through my work: how do living systems come to understand and act in the world — and how can machines learn to do the same? I approach intelligence the way a systems biologist approaches a cell: as a stochastic, multiscale, adaptive dynamical system.

Training Δ₀, a humanoid foundation model for whole-body loco-manipulation. Delta is incubated by the Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI); I work with Dr. Xiaojian Ma, Dr. Siyuan Huang, and Dr. Baoxiong Jia.
